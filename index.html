🔬 Research se — viral thumbnail ke 5 rules:
  • Face + emotion: strong emotion wala face CTR ko 25–40% tak badhata hai; face frame ka 20–35% hona chahiye.
  • 2–3 shabd: 10,000 thumbnails ke analysis mein 0–2 shabd wale thumbnails ka avg CTR 6.8% tha, 6+ shabd wale ka sirf 4.3%.
  • High contrast: peela/safed bold text + kaala outline, dark background par bright subject — phone par bhi padhne layak.
  • Ek hi kahani: zyada se zyada 2–3 elements — face + text + ek object. 3 se zyada cheezein CTR ~23% gira deti hain.
  • Face par text kabhi nahi: text hamesha face se door, aur neeche-right corner (duration badge) khaali rakho.
Sources: 2025–26 creator studies (VidIQ, TubeBuddy, 10k-thumbnail analysis, 300k viral videos dataset). Yeh patterns hain, guarantee nahi — thumbnail + title ka promise video mein poora hona chahiye.

Topic aur category chuno, Generate dabao. Har hook ke saath uska pattern likha hai (jaise Number Contrast, Question) taaki aap seekho ki kaunsa pattern kyun kaam karta hai. Thumbnail Maker mein photo kahan se laayein?

Apni khud ki photo ya video ka screenshot use karo — wahi sabse best hai. Doosre creators ki photo ya film ke stills use mat karo, woh copyright issue de sakte hain. Photo aapke phone/browser se bahar kahin nahi jaati.
